Scientific Data & Acoustic Sonification

Temperature: maps and values

A temperature anomaly shows how much warmer or cooler a period was compared with the average from 1951 to 1980. Whole Earth uses the annual global values from NASA GISS GISTEMP v4. Selected-area values come from the regional grids.

Historical maps show running means from NASA’s Scientific Visualization Studio. The 2025 map and selected-area values use January–December means from the official NASA GISS 2° × 2° grid.

Temperature sets a synthesized note's frequency: f = clamp(180 × 4^((anomaly + 0.8) / 3), 150, 850) Hz. Higher values produce higher notes.

Rainfall: a designed rain texture

The inherited rainfall grid is labelled GPCP in this project. Its provenance and processing still need a scientific audit. The selected value and its range shape filtered noise and scheduled droplets. Relative scaling can make small differences subtle. This is synthesized sound, not a weather recording.

Regions and years

Geographic bounds select a rectangle. Grid cells are weighted by their overlap and latitude area. The annual temperature archive covers 1980–2025. The rainfall archive covers 1980–2024.
